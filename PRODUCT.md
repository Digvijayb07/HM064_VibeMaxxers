# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Companies looking to hire practical, job-ready development talent via direct project challenges, and developers/freelancers looking to showcase their capabilities by submitting proposals and technical solutions.

## Product Purpose
TalentHub is a project-based collaboration platform that connects companies with developers through transparent, skill-driven workflows, replacing traditional resume screening with real work evaluation.

## Brand Personality
Precision, Technical, Restrained. The platform presents an aesthetic of a high-end developer HUD/console: clean blueprint-cyan grid structures, monospace metadata tags, hairline telemetry brackets on cards, and elegant typography accents using Outfit and Lora serif.

## Anti-references
Saturated SaaS cream/sand/beige neutral backgrounds (warm-neutral body backgrounds), text gradients, side-stripe card accent borders, over-rounded card corners (>12-16px), and soft drop shadows combined with borders on the same element.

## Design Principles
1. **Developer-HUD Precision**: Spacing, borders, and layouts should feel like precision telemetry equipment—functional, crisp, and clean.
2. **Transparent Clarity**: Information architecture must prioritize clarity and accessibility above decoration, ensuring no content is gated behind arbitrary UI elements.
3. **Contrast & Readability**: Maintain solid WCAG AA compliance (contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1) for all text and interactive controls, avoiding low-contrast grays.
4. **Motion with Purpose**: Transitions must reinforce spatial continuity (such as text-morphs on header navigation) and provide instant crossfade fallbacks for reduced-motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG AA contrast compliance for all text.
- Full respect for user reduced-motion preferences (`@media (prefers-reduced-motion)`).
- Semantic HTML markup for screen reader friendliness and accessible keyboard navigation.
